All 6,144 full positional workspaces in the invoice and file-fed map
complete in the native prefix. Each copies one evaluated argument.
The shipment variants each copy 13 slots into their one fresh
continuation. Counts cover the native entry paths, with general
interpreter entry and CLR recursion depth outside instrumentation.

Treat smaller continuations as a separate problem. Native operand
transport work remains in the invoice's `price_line(row)` and
file-fed map's `quantity(row)`. The ordinary rules, defaulted
rules and shipment callback variants are controls for that work.
The defaulted-rule gap still has its own construction and admission
profile. The current counts support comparing a replacement for the
full workspace handoff on supported positional calls. They give no
speedup estimate.
