## Bounded refinement decision

B386 text is consistently adverse. The new shared-prefix formatting locals also
conflict with its existing temporary-value lifetime fences. One source-justified
refinement keeps direct instructions, but moves their bodies into an ordinary
non-inlined helper called only for formatting opcodes. It removes no additional
checks and restores no generic prepared-operation or record classification.
This may reduce shared-loop register/code pressure; it is a hypothesis about the
timing cause, not established CPU attribution. Preserve the entire initial source,
images and observations. Recheck focused semantics/ownership and repeat the same
bounded comparison once on changed source; then decide retention and remaining
debt explicitly. No unchanged favorable retiming or fixed percentage veto.
